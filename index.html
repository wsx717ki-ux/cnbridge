Why it matters

Safety, not just style.
In a wallet UI, a word like approval or surplus translated literally reads wrong, and these strings ask users to sign transactions. We follow a security-first glossary.
PR-ready.
We keep Markdown, code blocks, commands, links, ICU plurals and {placeholders} intact, and deliver README.zh-CN.md, .po or .json catalogs you can merge.
AI speed, native review.
We draft with AI, then a native Simplified Chinese speaker reviews every line. No "blind" machine output, which most bounty and grant rules forbid anyway.
